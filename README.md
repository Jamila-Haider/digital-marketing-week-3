## Key Learnings
-Every piece of content should serve a specific stage of the marketing funnel — awareness, consideration, or conversion
-AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) and PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution) frameworks make copywriting measurably stronger
-Brand voice is who you always are; tone is how you speak in a specific situation — both must be defined before content creation
-AI generates fast but generic output — human cultural knowledge, emotional intelligence, and brand personality are irreplaceable at the refinement stage
-Visual consistency (same colors, fonts, style) across every post is what makes a brand look professional and recognizable
-Content pillars give every post a purpose — no random posting
